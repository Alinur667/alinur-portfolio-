02 / BACKGROUND

A little about
the person behind it.

My path began in the humanities. Engineering gave that curiosity a new direction.

At Abai National School, I shifted from humanities and political interests toward systems engineering. I taught myself full-assembly CAD in Fusion 360 through MIT OpenCourseWare, learning to build under real constraints.

My interests connect robotics, nuclear engineering and research at the INP with exploration of extreme environments. Beyond engineering, I am an active volunteer with the League of Volunteers of Kazakhstan.
